Inner Lotus — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

App: Inner Lotus (com.sjpixels.innerlotus) for Android

Developer: sjpixels — https://sjpixels.com/

In short

Inner Lotus works completely offline. It has no servers, no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not request the Internet permission. Everything you create stays inside the app on your device. We never receive, see, sell or share your data.

What the app stores, and where

The following is saved only in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Your own affirmations, favourites and affirmation settings
  • Journal entries, moods and dates, plus an optional journal PIN, stored only as a salted one-way hash
  • Habits and the days you complete them
  • Saved sound mixes
  • App settings such as language, form of address, voice speed, background, reminders and card style

This data is not included in Android cloud backups or device-to-device transfers. The app turns those off so your journal never leaves the phone without your action.

App usage statistics (optional)

The Habit Tracker can show how much time you spend in other apps. This feature works only if you turn on Usage access for Inner Lotus in Android settings, after an in-app explanation. The figures are read from Android's own usage statistics when you open that screen. They are shown to you and are never stored outside the app or sent anywhere. You can turn access off at any time in Android settings.

Text-to-speech

Read-aloud uses the text-to-speech engine already installed on your phone, such as Google Speech Services or Samsung TTS. Inner Lotus passes the text to be spoken to that engine on the device. Please refer to your TTS engine's own policy for how it operates.

Notifications

If you turn on daily reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. Nothing is sent over a network.

Files you export or share

The app writes data out only when you ask it to. This happens when you:

  • export a backup or file (JSON, CSV, TXT),
  • share an affirmation card or text with another app, or
  • save a card image to your Pictures folder.

Where that file goes next is controlled by you and by the app or location you choose. When you import a file, it is read from the location you pick and merged into the app's local storage.

Links

The app contains links to https://sjpixels.com/, to our Mantra Mala app on Google Play and to crisis helplines. Tapping one opens your browser, Play Store or phone dialer. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

You can delete journal entries, habits, mixes and your own affirmations in the app, or erase everything with Settings → Delete all my data. Uninstalling the app also deletes all of its data from the device.

Children

Inner Lotus is a general-audience wellness app. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone.

Health disclaimer

Inner Lotus offers self-care and wellness content. It does not provide medical, psychological or financial advice and is not a substitute for professional care. If you are in crisis, please contact your local emergency number or a crisis helpline.
