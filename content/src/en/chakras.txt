# Seven chakras, root to crown. seeds[0] is the classic poster affirmation.

chakra.root.name = Root
chakra.root.sanskrit = Muladhara
chakra.root.core = I AM
chakra.root.location = Base of the spine
chakra.root.element = Earth
chakra.root.bija = LAM
chakra.root.theme = Safety, grounding, stability, belonging
chakra.root.balanced = Feeling safe, grounded, secure and present in your body.
chakra.root.imbalanced = Fear, anxiety, money worries, restlessness, feeling disconnected.
chakra.root.practice = Walk barefoot on the earth, eat root vegetables, stand in mountain pose and chant LAM.
chakra.root.seeds:
- I am safe, centred and grounded.
- I am rooted like an ancient tree.
- The earth supports me with every step.
- I trust that all my needs are met.
- I belong here, in this body and this moment.
- I stand steady and strong.

chakra.sacral.name = Sacral
chakra.sacral.sanskrit = Svadhisthana
chakra.sacral.core = I FEEL
chakra.sacral.location = Lower belly, below the navel
chakra.sacral.element = Water
chakra.sacral.bija = VAM
chakra.sacral.theme = Emotions, creativity, pleasure, flow
chakra.sacral.balanced = Emotional flow, creativity, healthy pleasure and intimacy.
chakra.sacral.imbalanced = Numbness, guilt, creative blocks, mood swings.
chakra.sacral.practice = Dance, swim, try hip-opening yoga, express yourself creatively and chant VAM.
chakra.sacral.seeds:
- I am in touch with my feelings.
- My emotions flow freely like water.
- I welcome joy and pleasure into my life.
- My creativity flows with ease.
- I honour my desires and my body.
- I embrace change with grace.

chakra.solar.name = Solar Plexus
chakra.solar.sanskrit = Manipura
chakra.solar.core = I DO
chakra.solar.location = Upper belly, above the navel
chakra.solar.element = Fire
chakra.solar.bija = RAM
chakra.solar.theme = Willpower, confidence, action, self-worth
chakra.solar.balanced = Confidence, motivation, clear decisions and healthy boundaries.
chakra.solar.imbalanced = Low self-esteem, procrastination, need for control, tension in the stomach.
chakra.solar.practice = Strengthen your core, enjoy morning sunlight, eat warm foods and chant RAM.
chakra.solar.seeds:
- I am powerful, strong and confident.
- I act with courage and clarity.
- My inner fire fuels my purpose.
- I make decisions with ease.
- I am worthy of my dreams.
- I honour my own power.

chakra.heart.name = Heart
chakra.heart.sanskrit = Anahata
chakra.heart.core = I LOVE
chakra.heart.location = Centre of the chest
chakra.heart.element = Air
chakra.heart.bija = YAM
chakra.heart.theme = Love, compassion, forgiveness, connection
chakra.heart.balanced = Kindness, forgiveness, open-hearted connection and self-love.
chakra.heart.imbalanced = Grief, resentment, loneliness, difficulty trusting.
chakra.heart.practice = Practise gratitude and Ho'oponopono, open the chest with gentle backbends, spend time in nature and chant YAM.
chakra.heart.seeds:
- I am worthy of love.
- My heart is open to give and receive.
- I forgive myself and others with ease.
- Love flows through me and around me.
- I treat myself with kindness and compassion.
- I am connected to all beings through love.

chakra.throat.name = Throat
chakra.throat.sanskrit = Vishuddha
chakra.throat.core = I SPEAK
chakra.throat.location = Throat and neck
chakra.throat.element = Ether (space)
chakra.throat.bija = HAM
chakra.throat.theme = Communication, truth, self-expression
chakra.throat.balanced = Speaking honestly, listening well, creative expression.
chakra.throat.imbalanced = Fear of speaking up, over-talking, a tight throat or jaw.
chakra.throat.practice = Sing, hum, journal, speak your truth kindly and chant HAM.
chakra.throat.seeds:
- I hear and speak my truth.
- My voice is clear and kind.
- I express myself freely and honestly.
- I listen deeply and speak wisely.
- My words create beauty and peace.
- It is safe for me to be heard.

chakra.thirdeye.name = Third Eye
chakra.thirdeye.sanskrit = Ajna
chakra.thirdeye.core = I SEE
chakra.thirdeye.location = Between the eyebrows
chakra.thirdeye.element = Light
chakra.thirdeye.bija = OM
chakra.thirdeye.theme = Intuition, insight, imagination, clarity
chakra.thirdeye.balanced = Clear thinking, trust in your intuition, vivid imagination.
chakra.thirdeye.imbalanced = Confusion, overthinking, poor focus, feeling cut off from inner guidance.
chakra.thirdeye.practice = Meditate, visualise, keep a dream journal, practise alternate-nostril breathing and chant OM.
chakra.thirdeye.seeds:
- I trust my intuition.
- I see clearly, beyond the surface.
- My inner wisdom guides me.
- I am open to insight and vision.
- My mind is calm and clear.
- I trust what my inner eye reveals.

chakra.crown.name = Crown
chakra.crown.sanskrit = Sahasrara
chakra.crown.core = I KNOW
chakra.crown.location = Top of the head
chakra.crown.element = Thought and pure consciousness
chakra.crown.bija = Silence (or OM)
chakra.crown.theme = Spirit, connection, peace, wisdom
chakra.crown.balanced = Inner peace, a sense of meaning, connection to the Divine.
chakra.crown.imbalanced = Feeling lost, cynicism, spiritual disconnection.
chakra.crown.practice = Sit in silent meditation, pray, practise gratitude and rest in stillness.
chakra.crown.seeds:
- I am divine.
- I am connected to the infinite source.
- I am one with the universe.
- Divine light flows through me.
- I trust the wisdom of life.
- I am at peace with all that is.
