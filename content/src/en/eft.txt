# EFT tapping: points, techniques, 9-gamut steps and 12 guided topics.
# Each topic: setup (3, said on Karate Chop), round1 (8 reminder phrases, TH..UA), round2 (8 positive phrases)

eft.point.kc.name = Karate Chop
eft.point.kc.where = The fleshy outer edge of the hand, between the wrist and the little finger.
eft.point.th.name = Top of Head
eft.point.th.where = The crown, at the centre top of your head.
eft.point.eb.name = Eyebrow
eft.point.eb.where = Where the eyebrow begins, just above and to the side of the nose.
eft.point.se.name = Side of Eye
eft.point.se.where = On the bone at the outer corner of the eye.
eft.point.ue.name = Under Eye
eft.point.ue.where = On the bone under the eye, in line with the pupil.
eft.point.un.name = Under Nose
eft.point.un.where = The small area between the nose and the upper lip.
eft.point.ch.name = Chin
eft.point.ch.where = In the crease halfway between the lower lip and the chin.
eft.point.cb.name = Collarbone
eft.point.cb.where = Just below the inner ends of the collarbones, about an inch from the centre.
eft.point.ua.name = Under Arm
eft.point.ua.where = On the side of the body, about four inches below the armpit.
eft.point.gp.name = Gamut Point
eft.point.gp.where = On the back of the hand, in the groove between the ring and little finger knuckles.

eft.tech.basic.name = Basic Recipe
eft.tech.basic.desc = The classic method: set-up on the Karate Chop point, a round that acknowledges the feeling, then a positive round. Best for everyday use.
eft.tech.full.name = Full Recipe with 9 Gamut
eft.tech.full.desc = Adds Gary Craig's 9 Gamut procedure between the two rounds — eye movements, humming and counting that engage both sides of the brain.
eft.tech.gentle.name = Gentle Touch & Breathe
eft.tech.gentle.desc = Instead of tapping, rest your fingertips on each point and take one slow, full breath. Ideal at night, in public, or when you feel very sensitive.
eft.tech.positive.name = Positive Round
eft.tech.positive.desc = Tap through the points using only positive statements, to anchor calm and confidence after you already feel better.

eft.gamut:
- Close your eyes.
- Open your eyes.
- Keeping your head still, look hard down to the right.
- Now look hard down to the left.
- Roll your eyes in a full circle.
- Roll your eyes in a circle the other way.
- Hum a few seconds of a happy tune.
- Count quickly from one to five.
- Hum the tune again.
