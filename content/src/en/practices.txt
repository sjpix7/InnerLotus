Your data stays with you.

Inner Lotus works completely offline. It has no servers, no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your journals, habits, affirmations, mixes and settings are stored only inside the app on this device.

Nothing is uploaded or shared unless you choose to export a file or share a card yourself.

App-usage insights in the Habit Tracker are read from Android's own usage statistics, only after you allow access, and are never stored outside the app or sent anywhere.

Uninstalling the app deletes your data, so export a backup first if you want to keep it.
